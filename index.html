About this map

Westport in 3D, built on the Westport Atlas: every street, building, shoreline and address comes from the Atlas files (gaz.json and wpdata.json), at true scale in meters from Courthouse Square.

The Nine's homes and the town's landmarks are modeled in Blender from your photos and Pinterest board, and placed on their exact Atlas footprints. Every other building is drawn from its Atlas footprint and height.

How to move

  • Left-drag pans, right-drag turns, the wheel zooms toward the cursor.
  • 2D looks straight down with north up. Street drops you to eye level: drag to look, W A S D or the arrow keys to walk, Shift to run, Esc to leave.
  • Press / to search and M to measure.

What is canon

Cards show each place's status from your register. Designs of the homes are interpretations unless a photo was supplied; the Map check lists everything that was corrected or needs your decision.
