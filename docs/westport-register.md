# Westport

This register is written to the author, so "you" and "your" in it mean the author.

The written companion to the Westport Atlas (https://claude.ai/artifact/PmUtdjnwRgDswxyJbfLC6D). Every address below is a real building on that map. Nothing marked "proposed" is canon until you confirm it.

## What the map is

- **Ground:** an invented coastline drawn to read like the Carolina coast (your pick, 2026-10-17), with no real town under it. The Westport River splits the town north and south and opens into the harbor. A barrier island with Westport Beach sits across the water, and the Atlantic lies beyond it.
- **Names:** all fictional (your pick). Westport is the seat of Lindon County, and the main highway is Ocean Highway with no route number (both your calls, 2026-10-17).
- **Scale:** about 38,000 buildings. In town that is roughly 22,000 single-family houses, 1,470 duplexes, 331 apartment buildings (about 7,900 units in 34 complexes plus the Pier Apartments), 58 townhouse rows, about 2,500 mobile homes in parks, and 1,259 downtown storefronts with apartments over many of them. At 2.4 people a household and 10% vacancy that houses about 78,000 people, a little over your 75,000. The beach town (about 2,650 cottages) and the rural roads outside the limits are extra.
- **Benchmarks:** 10 elementary schools, 3 middle schools, Westport High with a freshman campus, 7 fire stations, about 40 churches on their own lots (real towns this size have 90 to 130, so many more sit in storefronts and houses), Westport Hospital, a community college, the port and rail yard. Sized against real NC cities of 55,000 to 95,000 (Gastonia, Rocky Mount, Jacksonville, Greenville).
- **Addresses** start at Courthouse Square and climb outward, about 1,000 numbers per mile, odd on one side and even on the other, the way most NC towns number.

## Canon placements this map follows

- Canon 2 fixed five households, and the map uses them as written: Monet on Oak Street in the Garden District; the Thompsons on Pelican Point Drive, the Patels on Sandpiper Lane and the Al-Hassans on Spinnaker Court, all in Harborview; the Hernandez house on Mercer Street; the Abernathy trailer at lot 47 of Riverside Mobile Home Community.
- **The Standing Stones and the nine acres** are inside the city limits, east of the river mouth (Braxton's landing), per the lock. The field has a tree line, a creek, a field gate on Chadwick Road, the rec center soccer fields behind the trees, and Royce's waterfront lots beside it. Canon 2 had them out past the quarry; that is out of date. The quarry stays on Quarry Road, outside the limits.
- **Westport High** sits on Patriots Boulevard, which now runs through the middle of the north side so Canon 2's commutes come close: Dalton's trailer to school is about 5 miles by road over the Dalton Bridge (Canon 2 says 4), Charlie's drive from Pelican Point Drive is about 4 miles, 8 to 9 minutes, and their two homes sit about 5.3 road miles apart (Canon 2 says 6.3).
- **The pier and the pier bar** (locked) stand out over the downtown harbor, near the bait shop and the crab house. **The water tower** carries FOUNDED 1625 (locked).
- **The marsh** runs up a tidal creek behind the trailer park, as the lock says. The trailer park is in Riverside, not literally behind the port (the port is two miles east); see the open calls if that matters to you.
